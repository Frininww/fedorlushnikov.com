Story

Short of breath, then Player of the Month.

I started playing soccer at 13 with asthma. I could not run half a mile without stopping, while my teammates kept the tempo without effort.

So I built a plan. I ran laps before every practice, jogged 4 to 7 kilometers every weekend and tracked my goals and weak spots on a board. After about two months I could keep up with the team. In the 2022/23 season my coaches named me Player of the Month.

That board is how I think about code. A product is a long list of small tasks, and finishing them one by one gets you somewhere real. Managing my own health made me want to build tools that help other people do the same.

App ecosystem · Grades 10-11

Serenity

serenityapps.online · grew out of @HealthScheduled_Bot

An ecosystem of calm apps for studying, planning and self-care, which I am building at Skolkovo. It grew out of my Telegram health bot HealthScheduled, now Serene Vita. I planned the bot for a month with my Social Studies teacher, surveyed students and teachers, and wrote its check-up with a strength and conditioning coach who worked with Russian soccer clubs. Then it grew into three apps on one account.

  1. Serene Vita on Telegram: a 21-question check-up, a personal daily plan, goals, streaks and levels, in Russian, English and Spanish.
  2. Serene Schedule in the browser: notes, tasks, kanban boards, lecture recording with transcripts and AI summaries. Data stays on your device.
  3. Serenity Assistant: one AI assistant that sees your tasks and your health goals, works by text or voice and changes things only after you say yes.
  4. One account: sign in once with Telegram or email, and it works in every Serenity app.

Desktop app · Grade 11

LockedIn

Built for my school's computer labs

Students in my school's computer labs kept playing games instead of following the lesson. I talked to our CS teacher and built an app that blocks games for a focus session of any length.

  • Detects and blocks more than 1,300 known games while a session runs
  • Session length is adjustable, for a lesson or a long study block
  • Built in Python with Flet, code on GitHub
